# Ola Digital — Competitor & Market Research Prompts

**Company:** Ola Digital
**Type:** Digital marketing / web agency
**Market:** Olavarría, Buenos Aires Province, Argentina (local market)

---

## Prompt 101 · Competitor Battlecard

Analyze **[COMPETITOR NAME]** from the perspective of **Ola Digital**, a digital marketing and web agency based in Olavarría, Argentina.

Cover:
- **Positioning** — how they present themselves and to whom
- **Pricing** — packages, ranges, payment terms (if public)
- **Strengths** — what they do well, where they win
- **Weaknesses** — gaps Ola Digital could exploit
- **What they're likely doing next** — moves to anticipate

Format as a one-page battlecard I can use in sales conversations. Include a "How Ola Digital wins" section at the bottom with 3 concrete talking points.

---

## Prompt 102 · Market Sizing

Estimate the TAM, SAM, and SOM for **digital marketing services in Olavarría, Argentina**.

For each:
- Walk through the calculation step by step
- State assumptions explicitly (population, business count, average spend, etc.)
- Cite data sources where possible (INDEC, local chambers of commerce, etc.)
- Run a sensitivity analysis (best/likely/worst case)

Flag where estimates are most uncertain and what data would tighten them.

End with a one-paragraph "What this means for Ola Digital" takeaway — realistic revenue ceiling and where to focus first.

---

## Prompt 103 · SWOT Analysis

Run a SWOT analysis for **Ola Digital** (digital marketing agency, Olavarría, Argentina).

For each quadrant:
- Rank items by impact (high / medium / low)
- Identify second-order effects (what does each item *lead to*?)
- Suggest specific actions Ola Digital should take in the next 90 days

Go beyond surface observations. Push back on obvious answers. End with the single biggest strategic risk and the single biggest opportunity.

---

## How to use these

1. Paste any prompt above into Claude (or run via Claude Code) when you need that analysis
2. For Prompt 101, fill in `[COMPETITOR NAME]` with each competitor one at a time
3. Re-run quarterly — markets shift, especially small local ones
