## Part 1 — Top 5 local competitors

### 1. Studio Concept
- **Site/office:** [studioconcept.com.ar](https://studioconcept.com.ar/) · Moreno 3145, Olavarría
- **Services:** Graphic design, web design, vehicle/adhesive graphics, signage, corporate identity, large-format printing
- **Positioning:** Full-service "communication studio" — integrated print + digital + signage. 10+ years.
- **Strengths:** Physical premises, long tenure, broad service mix, established SMB relationships
- **Weaknesses:** Print-heavy DNA; likely weak on paid social, analytics, e-commerce, automation
- **Likely next move:** Defend installed base with print+web bundles; slow to add modern paid-media

### 2. 3D Gráfica
- **Site:** [3d-grafica.com.ar](https://www.3d-grafica.com.ar/) · phone (02284) 414-510
- **Services:** Naming, isotype/logo, institutional identity, web design + development
- **Positioning:** Branding-led design studio (~10 years)
- **Strengths:** Brand/identity craft, local roots, SMB portfolio
- **Weaknesses:** Light on performance marketing, no e-commerce specialty, dated site
- **Likely next move:** Continue identity+WordPress combos; minimal expansion

### 3. DH Multimedios
- **Site:** [dhmultimedios.com](https://dhmultimedios.com/)
- **Services:** Software, websites, mobile apps, brand design
- **Positioning:** Dev shop with design ambitions — more technical than marketing-led
- **Strengths:** Software/app capability (rare locally), good for custom dashboards/booking systems
- **Weaknesses:** Thin marketing-services bench, sparse messaging, light portfolio
- **Likely next move:** Lean into custom-software; partner rather than compete on marketing

### 4. Samalo
- **Site:** [samalo.com.ar](https://samalo.com.ar/) — *site unreachable at research time (red flag)*
- **Services (per listings):** Web design, communications, marketing for SMB
- **Positioning:** "Young professionals, original solutions" — small modern studio
- **Strengths:** Fresh aesthetic, likely lower prices, agile
- **Weaknesses:** Site downtime, thin case studies, durability unclear
- **Likely next move:** Compete on price + modern look; vulnerable to a better-marketed peer

### 5. Digital Olavarría / Gráfica G
- **Site:** [graficag.com.ar/digital-olavarria/](https://graficag.com.ar/digital-olavarria/) · César Milstein 5570
- **Services:** Digital arm of an established print shop
- **Positioning:** Print-house extending into digital; bundles work with traditional production
- **Strengths:** Backed by established print parent (cash flow, client list, physical presence)
- **Weaknesses:** Likely side-arm not dedicated team; digital depth limited
- **Likely next move:** Cross-sell digital to existing print clients; slow to win net-new

### Out-of-region pricing pressure
[Siarweb (Misiones)](https://www.siarweb.com/diseno-web-olavarria-buenos-aires), [EfeMosse (CABA)](https://www.efemossesistemas.com.ar/), and [Tradeweb](https://tradeweb.com.ar/diseno-web-en-olavarria-buenos-aires-argentina/) target Olavarría remotely — they cap commodity web-build pricing but rarely win the relationship sale.

## Part 3 — Market sizing (Olavarría digital marketing)

### Inputs and assumptions
- **Olavarría businesses:** ~6,500 ([Municipalidad 2023](https://www.olavarria.gov.ar/olavarria-cuenta-con-6500-empresas-y-comercios/)). Mix: 3,690 retail, 1,355 services, 643 industrial, ~810 other. >60% micro/small.
- **Argentina digital ad spend 2025:** ~AR$ 791B (46.6% of AR$ 1.696T total media — [CAAM via CAA](https://anunciantes.org.ar/caam-inversion-publicitaria-medios/))
- **Population share weighting:** Olavarría 0.24% of Argentina × 1.3× industrial intensity = ~0.31% of national digital spend
- **Agency revenue conversion:** typical 20–30% of media spend + standalone web/branding budgets
- **SMB pricing benchmarks 2026** (sources: [Web360](https://web360.com/blog/marketing-digital/cuanto-cuesta-contratar-una-agencia-de-marketing-digital-en-argentina-guia-2026/), [agenciadigital.com.ar](https://agenciadigital.com.ar/tarifario-marketing-digital-argentina/)): small AR$ 500k/mo, mid AR$ 1.0–1.5M/mo; standalone services AR$ 300–500k/mo
- **Excluded:** Loma Negra, Cementos Avellaneda and other large industrials (national agency buyers)

### TAM (agency revenue opportunity — not media pass-through)

| Scenario | Annual TAM |
|---|---|
| Worst | ~AR$ 700M (~USD 700k) |
| Likely | ~AR$ 1.1B (~USD 1.1M) |
| Best | ~AR$ 1.7B (~USD 1.7M) |

### SAM (SMBs realistically buying from a local agency)
~6,500 businesses × ~25% with budget+intent = ~1,600 prospects. Only ~10–15% actively buying in a given year = ~200 active. Blended ARPU ≈ AR$ 4.3M/year per account.

| Scenario | Annual SAM |
|---|---|
| Worst | ~AR$ 500M |
| Likely | ~AR$ 860M |
| Best | ~AR$ 1.3B |

### SOM (one mid-tier agency, 3-year capture)
Targeting 8–15% share of SAM:

| Scenario | Year-3 SOM | Active accounts |
|---|---|---|
| Worst | AR$ 50M (~USD 50k) | 12–15 |
| Likely | AR$ 110M (~USD 110k) | 25–30 |
| Best | AR$ 200M (~USD 200k) | 45–55 |

### What this means for Ola Digital
The Olavarría agency market is **small in absolute terms (low-to-mid six figures USD) but structurally underserved at the SMB level** — incumbents are print/branding-led with weak performance benches. A focused productized retainer offer (paid social + WhatsApp funnels + monthly reporting) can plausibly reach ~AR$ 100M/year in three years on 25–30 accounts. **The constraint is talent supply and packaging discipline, not demand.**

## Part 4 — SWOT

### Strengths

| Item | Impact | Second-order effect |
|---|---|---|
| Local presence in a small city — face-to-face trust matters | **H** | Lower CAC than out-of-town; word-of-mouth compounds in 110k city |
| Likely younger/digitally-native team vs. print-led incumbents | **M** | Faster turnaround, better content fluency, credible Instagram-led pitches |
| Low cost base (Olavarría salaries < CABA) | **M** | Can underprice CABA agencies with >40% margins |

### Weaknesses

| Item | Impact | Second-order effect |
|---|---|---|
| Zero brand discoverability (no SEO/site visible) | **H** | Inbound is purely referral — fragile + capacity-bound |
| Likely tiny team (1–5 inferred) | **H** | Can't service >20 retainers without founder burnout; bus-factor risk |
| No public case studies or metrics | **M** | Hard to close mid-tier clients who expect ROI proof |
| Limited specialist depth (paid media, SEO, video) | **M** | Generalist trap — loses bake-offs to specialists |

### Opportunities

| Item | Impact | Second-order effect |
|---|---|---|
| Incumbents weak on performance marketing | **H** | "Performance + reporting" position is open and defensible |
| ~5,000 SMBs without sophisticated digital presence | **H** | Productized "Plan Pyme" at AR$ 200–300k/mo scales via referrals |
| WhatsApp Business + Meta Ads underused locally | **M** | Differentiated lead-gen offer competitors don't sell |
| Industrial supplier ecosystem (around Loma Negra) unaddressed for B2B | **M** | B2B sub-brand could win AR$ 800k+/mo tickets |

### Threats

| Item | Impact | Second-order effect |
|---|---|---|
| Argentine macro: inflation + FX kills SMB budgets fast | **H** | Retainer churn spikes in downturns; need 6+ mo contracts with price clauses |
| Out-of-region web shops cap commodity pricing | **M** | Race-to-bottom on commodity sites; must move clients to retainers fast |
| Talent flight to CABA/remote USD wages | **H** | Hiring cost rises faster than client willingness-to-pay |
| Meta/Google reducing organic reach → clients blame agency | **M** | Misaligned expectations → churn; needs upfront education |

### Three concrete 90-day actions

1. **Publish 6 case studies + productized pricing page on a fast site.** Pick 6 wins across 2–3 verticals (gastronomy, retail, professional services). One-page site with "Plan Crecimiento" at AR$ 250k/mo and "Plan Performance" at AR$ 600k/mo. Closes discoverability gap; forces packaging discipline.
2. **Launch "WhatsApp + Meta Ads" lead-gen offer for retail/gastronomy.** Sell on outcome (qualified WhatsApp conversations/month). Target 10 sign-ups in 90 days at AR$ 250–350k/mo. Produces visible wins (every restaurant owner knows the others).
3. **Hire or contract one paid-media specialist** (fractional/remote OK). Without this, account capacity caps at ~10 retainers and the agency can't scale past ~AR$ 30M/year.

### Biggest risk + biggest opportunity

- **Biggest risk:** Macro-driven SMB budget cuts in a city tied to the construction/cement cycle. [Loma Negra is currently shutting its main kiln due to demand collapse (Infobae, May 2026)](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/05/12/loma-negra-golpeada-por-la-caida-de-la-actividad-la-cementera-mas-grande-del-pais-apagara-su-principal-horno-hasta-noviembre/) — when the plant cuts back, local SMBs feel it in 60 days. **Mitigation:** lock 6-month retainers with price-adjustment clauses; over-index counter-cyclical verticals (health, dental, education, food).
- **Biggest opportunity:** No competitor in Olavarría visibly owns "performance marketing with monthly reporting." Whoever credibly claims this in the next 12 months becomes the default SMB referral. In a city of 110k, that's a winner-take-most position worth 25–40 retainers.
