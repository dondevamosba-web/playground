**Mode**: Active monetization while systems run. You do the sales, automation does the posting.

## 💼 Sponsor Outreach (Your Job)

### Targets (High ROI)

**Tier 1 - Big Festivals** ($1,500-3,000/placement):
- Creamfields Argentina
- Fauna Primavera
- Ultra (if coming)
- **Pitch**: "Exclusive announces to 15K+ BA electronic audience"

**Tier 2 - Venues** ($400-1,000/placement):
- Crobar, Club AMK, Mandarine Park
- Rio Electronic Music, Elements
- **Pitch**: "Promote your events to engaged electronic audience"

**Tier 3 - Artists/Collectives** ($200-500/placement):
- DJ duos, local producers
- **Pitch**: "Get your date in front of 15K+ followers"

### How to Pitch

**Email template**:
```
Subject: Featured on Fiestas Electronicas (15K followers, engaged BA audience)

Hi [Sponsor/Artist],

Fiestas Electronicas posts your event to 15K+ followers in Buenos Aires.
Our audience: electronic music fans, 18-35, high engagement.

Options:
- Event featured post ($500) — top of feed for 3 days
- Reel feature ($300) — 30-60s video of your event
- Story takeover ($200) — 24h stories from your account

Last month: 850K impressions, 45K engagements.

Want to discuss? Reply to this email.

— Guido
Fiestas Electronicas
```

### What to Offer

1. **Featured Post** ($500)
   - Your event gets priority (published first)
   - Pinned to followers' stories
   - Swipe-up link to your tickets

2. **Story Series** ($300)
   - 5-10 stories leading up to event
   - Countdown stickers
   - "Tickets link in bio"

3. **Reel Feature** ($300)
   - 30-60s video of your event
   - Highlights best moments
   - High engagement

4. **Exclusive Announce** ($200)
   - 48h early access
   - "Announced first on Fiestas"

### Timeline

**Month 1**: Reach out to 10 targets (festivals + 5 venues)
**Month 2**: Close 2-3 sponsors (= $800-1,500)
**Month 3**: Scale to 5-10/month

**Setup** (30 min):

2. Name: "Fiestas Electronicas Buenos Aires"
3. Bio: "Weekly exclusive events before IG announcement"
4. Pricing: Free + paid ($5/mo for early access)

**Content** (weekly):
- Tuesday 9 AM: "This week's top 3 events"
- Thursday: "Hot venue tips" (paid only)
- Friday: "Next week's full calendar" (paid early access)

**Estimate**: 500-1,000 free subs first month → 50-100 paid = $250-500/mo

### Option B: Patreon (Higher commitment)

**Setup** (45 min):
1. Create Patreon account
2. Tiers:
   - Free: Weekly highlights
   - $3/mo: 48h early event access
   - $10/mo: Private Discord (chat with other fans)

**Estimate**: Same conversion, but higher barrier to entry

### Option C: Both (Hybrid)

- Substack for free tier (build list)
- Patreon for paid tier (deeper engagement)
- Link both in Linktree

**Action**: Pick one this week. Start with Substack (easier).

## Red Flags (Stop & Adjust)

🚩 No sponsor replies after 10 emails
→ Adjust pitch, try different angle (pricing, bundle deals)

🚩 Email list stalled at <100 subs
→ Need more promotion (ask followers, IG story CTA)

🚩 Affiliate clicks not converting
→ Check: are links pointing to right events? Test URLs manually.
