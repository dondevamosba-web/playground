FAQ

Common questions.

How quickly will I start getting leads?

Most clients see their first leads within 7–10 days of campaign launch. We target an initial ramp of 2 weeks from strategy call to live ads.

Do I need to have run ads before?

No. We set up everything from scratch — ad accounts, pages, landing pages. All you need is a business and a target area.

What's the difference between the ad spend and the management fee?

The management fee is what you pay us. The ad spend goes directly to Facebook/Google — it never passes through us. You get full transparency on what's being spent and where.

What if I only want Meta ads, not Google?

The Starter plan is Meta-only. You can add Google at any point — just upgrade to Growth when you're ready to scale.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month means you can cancel with 30 days notice. No cancellation fees, no "early termination" clauses.

Do you work with commercial roofers too?

Yes — though commercial lead gen requires a different strategy (longer sales cycles, different platforms). Tell us on the call and we'll scope accordingly.
