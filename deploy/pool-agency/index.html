Pool leads on aggregator platforms are shared, expensive, and mostly not ready to build.

Lead marketplaces sell pool inquiries to every builder in your area simultaneously. Half those homeowners are just dreaming — and you're spending thousands in fees chasing people who won't pull the trigger for two years.

$200–$600 per lead, shared

FAQ

Common questions.

How quickly will I start getting leads?

Most clients see their first leads within 7–10 days. Pool campaigns ramp over 2–3 weeks as the algorithm finds your ideal buyer profile.

Do I need to have run ads before?

No. We build everything from scratch — ad accounts, landing pages, targeting. All you need is a licensed pool building business and a service area.

What's the difference between ad spend and the management fee?

The management fee is what you pay us. Ad spend goes directly to Facebook/Google — never through us. Full transparency on every dollar.

Pool jobs take months to close. How do you account for that?

We build remarketing sequences specifically for the long pool decision cycle. Leads who don't convert immediately are re-targeted with project galleries, financing options, and seasonal urgency messaging.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month means you can cancel with 30 days notice. No cancellation fees, no early termination clauses.

Do you handle pool remodel and renovation leads separately from new builds?

Yes. New construction and remodel buyers have completely different motivations and timelines. We build separate campaigns for each with dedicated landing pages.

When should I start campaigns to capture pre-season leads?

January and February are the highest-intent months in most markets. We recommend launching by mid-January to capture homeowners planning their summer builds before your competitors fill their calendars.
