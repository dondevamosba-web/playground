The Problem

Pest control growth stalls when you rely on referrals and shared-lead platforms.

HomeAdvisor and Angi sell your leads to three other companies before you even see the notification. And with pest control, the homeowner calls whoever responds fastest — usually your competitor.

🪲

Shared leads mean you're always racing to respond

A homeowner who just found a roach infestation wants someone on the phone in five minutes. When four companies get the same lead, the one who responds first wins — and that's rarely you.

FAQ

Common questions.

How quickly will I start getting leads?

Most clients see their first leads within 7–10 days of campaign launch. Spring and summer campaigns often ramp even faster due to high seasonal demand.

Do I need to have run ads before?

No. We build everything from scratch — ad accounts, landing pages, targeting. All you need is a licensed pest control business and a service area.

What's the difference between ad spend and the management fee?

The management fee is what you pay us. Ad spend goes directly to Facebook/Google — never through us. Full transparency on every dollar.

Can you focus on recurring contract signups vs. one-time calls?

Absolutely — and we recommend building both. One-time treatment campaigns fill your schedule short-term; recurring contract campaigns build predictable annual revenue. We run them separately with different messaging and landing pages.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month means you can cancel with 30 days notice. No cancellation fees, no early termination clauses.

Do you handle commercial pest control campaigns too?

Commercial accounts are high value but require a different channel mix (LinkedIn, direct outreach, Google Search). Tell us on the call and we'll scope accordingly.

Can you target specific pests — termites, rodents, bed bugs?

Yes. Pest-specific campaigns often outperform general pest control ads because the targeting and messaging match exactly what the homeowner is searching for.
